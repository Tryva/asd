# ASD prospecting notes

## Scope and ICM

This is a discovery knowledge base, not an outreach log. No person was contacted
in this task. Each target separates `OBSERVATION`, `EVIDENCE`, `HYPOTHESIS`, and
`DECISION`; inference is never presented as observed pain.

## Search patterns that worked

- GitHub topics and contributor graphs for `ai-agents`, `agentic-ai`,
  `multi-agent`, `orchestration`, and active framework repositories.
- Public issue/discussion searches for `stuck`, `loop`, `retry`, `repeated tool
  call`, `checkpoint`, `trace`, `eval`, `production`, and `cost`.
- Official team pages to verify identity and project ownership after discovery.
- HN searches for durable execution, guardrails, tool calling, and agent
  workflow reliability.
- Product Hunt AI workflow/observability pages for recent attributable makers.

## Failed or weak patterns

- Topic search alone returns many low-signal repositories and does not establish
  buyer intent.
- Unlinked Reddit usernames were not promoted to targets.
- Generic launch copy and reviews were retained as source context, not proof of
  pain or willingness to pay.
- SignalHire, JustRaised, ContactOut, Growjo, and similar data-broker results
  were rejected. No guessed personal emails were used.

## Recurring pain themes

Public material repeatedly points to repeated tool calls, retries, stuck or
drifting workflows, unclear state/checkpoint boundaries, missing progress
anchors, weak traceability, evaluation gaps, and compounding cost in multi-step
execution. These are fit hypotheses until confirmed directly with a user.

## Source quality

Highest-signal sources are active OSS repositories, issue/discussion history,
contributor graphs, and official pages that verify identity. Communities are
good discovery surfaces but need attribution and a concrete project. HN and
Product Hunt are useful for new pools but are marketing- or anecdote-weighted.

## Communities and future pools

Keep monitoring CrewAI Community, Pydantic community channels, Langfuse and
LlamaIndex GitHub discussions, Braintrust public community/SDK activity, HN,
and the three Reddit communities listed in `SOURCES.md`. Future pools can add
builders of coding agents, browser workflows, durable execution, agent SRE,
and self-hosted inference once public identity and fit are both verified.

## Human gate

This task stops at discovery and qualification. Outreach, use of Gmail, and any
provider call require a separate explicit approval. Generic team aliases may be
listed as public contact routes, but are not treated as individual emails.
